## How to Contribute

1. **Issues & Discussion:**
   - Found a bug or tested on different hardware? Feel free to open an Issue.
   - Have ideas for selective cgroup freezing? Join or start a discussion in the Issues tab.

2. **Pull Requests (PRs):**
   - Keep your code clean, concise, and documented.
   - Explain what your PR changes or fixes.
   - Submitting a PR implies full acceptance of the **Contribution Licensing Terms** stated above.
